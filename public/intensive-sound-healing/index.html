Frequently asked questions

Who is this masterclass for?

People interested in learning sound instruments, guided relaxation and responsible facilitation. Beginners should follow the instructor’s guidance and practise within their skill level.

Are all 100 modules shown here?

Yes. The complete numbered curriculum is available above, organised into four pathways with concise descriptions.

Is this a medical treatment or clinical qualification?

No medical treatment or clinical qualification is implied. Any certificate should reflect the actual training completed and the provider’s stated scope.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothing, a notebook, water and any items specified by the organiser. Confirm instrument arrangements before attending.
